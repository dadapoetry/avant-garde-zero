[CLOSE]

AG0 publishes theoretical texts, manifestos, poetic statements, and critical positions that articulate the need for a new international avant-garde. We are looking for contributions that take a position — that argue, provoke, and open a front.

Submissions in any language, up to ~5000 words, with or without images. Send your position as plain text to:

submit → avantgardezero@proton.me

Include a short bio (2-3 sentences). Simultaneous submissions accepted. AG0 is a non-profit, non-commercial publication. All rights remain with the author.
